This document sets out the rules that members must follow when using TestGym facilities. Failure to comply with these rules may result in cancellation of membership.

Members must familiarise themselves with the TestGym Gym Rules and the Safety and Security guidance, including the guidance applicable during unstaffed hours.

Members must provide identification (ID) when requested by a member of TestGym staff.

Members must wear clothing and footwear that are appropriate and suitable for exercise.

Members must ask a member of TestGym staff for assistance if they are unsure how to safely use any gym machine or piece of equipment.

Members must return weights and other gym equipment to their designated locations after use.

Members must be considerate of other members and avoid occupying gym machines or equipment for excessive periods.

Members must notify TestGym of any changes to their contact details, including email addresses and telephone numbers. Contact details can be updated through the Members' Area of the TestGym website.

Members must keep personal valuables with them while visiting the gym. Members are solely responsible for their personal belongings. TestGym accepts no responsibility for loss of or damage to personal property.

Members must use the changing-room facilities appropriate to their biological sex or use the available all-gender facilities.

Members must take personal responsibility for their training and safety and only perform activities that are within their physical ability and experience.

Members must not share their PIN number or QR code with another person or allow another person to use their membership access credentials.

Members must not allow another person to enter the gym by following them through an access-controlled entrance (tailgating). Any suspected tailgating should be reported to TestGym staff.

Members must not use gym machines or equipment when they are unsure how to operate them safely. Members should seek assistance from TestGym staff before using unfamiliar equipment.

Members must not use the gym when they are unwell or otherwise unable to exercise safely without supervision.

Members must not bring bags or large personal items onto the gym floor. Such items may create a safety risk, and TestGym staff may remove items that they believe present a danger to members.

Members must not wear inappropriate exercise clothing or footwear. Jeans, boots, flip-flops or sandals, slip-on shoes, workwear, and clothing that may reasonably cause offence to other members are not permitted on the gym floor.

Members must not train in large groups or occupy gym machines or equipment for excessive periods. During busy periods, members are encouraged to share equipment between sets.

Members must not use the gym while under the influence of alcohol, narcotics, tranquillisers, medication, or any other substance that may impair their ability to exercise safely. TestGym does not provide a general list of permitted substances; the key requirement is that members must be able to exercise safely.

Members must not bring illegal substances onto TestGym premises.

Members must not drop or misuse weights on the gym floor, except in areas specifically designed and designated for heavy lifting.

Members must not take photographs, record videos, or livestream other members without their permission.

Members must stop filming when requested by TestGym staff or another member to do so for privacy reasons.

Members must not make video calls in changing rooms.

Members must not behave aggressively, abusively, violently, or threateningly while on TestGym premises.

Members must not behave in an anti-social manner on TestGym premises. Prohibited behaviour includes making unwanted sexual advances towards other members or TestGym staff, staring at or catcalling other members or TestGym staff, or continuing to engage with another person after they have asked to be left alone.

Members must not use the gym if they are under 16 years of age. TestGym staff may request identification to verify a member's age.

Members must not smoke or use vapes or e-cigarettes anywhere on TestGym premises.

Members must not bring bicycles or e-scooters inside TestGym premises. Bicycles and e-scooters must be left outside the gym.

Members must not bring a personal trainer into the gym unless the trainer is a TestGym personal trainer or the member has received written permission from TestGym Central Support.

Members must not allow an unauthorised personal trainer to provide personal training services inside the gym. Any person providing personal training without the required TestGym authorisation may be asked to leave immediately.

Members must not bring pets onto TestGym premises, except for official assistance or aid dogs.

TestGym operates CCTV throughout all gyms 24 hours a day, 7 days a week for the purposes of member safety and security.

TestGym reserves the right to allow its employees and authorised contractors to review CCTV footage when required.

CCTV footage may be reviewed as part of an investigation into member complaints, incidents or safety issues, disputes between members, or other situations where reviewing footage is necessary to investigate an issue.

CCTV recordings may be used to assist TestGym in investigating complaints, incidents, disputes, or other relevant matters.

Members are required to comply with these Gym Rules while using TestGym facilities. If a member breaches the Gym Rules, TestGym may have the right to cancel the member's membership.
